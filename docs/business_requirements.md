# RetailPulse 360 — Business Requirements

## 1. Business Context

RetailPulse 360 is a fictional U.S. omnichannel e-commerce retailer. The company has experienced customer and transaction growth, but leadership needs better visibility into profitability, customer retention, marketing efficiency, refund behavior, and customer engagement.

The objective of this project is to build an end-to-end analytics solution that converts transactional, customer, digital-behavior, marketing, and support data into actionable business insights.

## 2. Analyst Role

As the Data Analyst, the responsibility is to:

* validate and prepare the available data;
* analyze business performance using SQL and Python;
* define and monitor key performance indicators;
* identify customer, product, channel, and regional trends;
* develop an executive Power BI dashboard;
* predict customers at risk of churn;
* identify actionable customer segments;
* translate analytical results into business recommendations.

## 3. Primary Stakeholders

### Executive Leadership

Needs a high-level view of growth, profitability, customer retention, and major business risks.

### Marketing Team

Needs to understand acquisition-channel performance, customer value, engagement, and opportunities for more efficient marketing allocation.

### Customer Retention / CRM Team

Needs to identify high-risk customers and understand which customer groups should receive different retention strategies.

### Customer Experience Team

Needs visibility into support-ticket volume, resolution time, refund requests, and customer satisfaction.

### Finance / Commercial Team

Needs reliable revenue, profit, margin, order-value, and category-performance metrics.

## 4. Core Business Questions

1. How are revenue, profit, orders, customers, and average order value changing over time?
2. Which product categories contribute the most revenue and profit?
3. Which U.S. regions produce the strongest commercial performance?
4. Which customer-acquisition channels generate valuable customers?
5. How do refunds and cancellations affect business performance?
6. What customer behaviors are associated with support demand and lower satisfaction?
7. What proportion of customers purchase repeatedly?
8. Which customers are most likely to churn in the next 90 days?
9. What behavioral and value-based customer segments exist?
10. What actions could improve customer retention, profitability, and marketing efficiency?

## 5. Analytical Deliverables

The final solution will include:

* data-quality assessment;
* SQL business analysis;
* Python exploratory data analysis;
* KPI and trend analysis;
* Power BI executive dashboard;
* supervised churn-classification model;
* unsupervised customer-segmentation model;
* model evaluation and business interpretation;
* evidence-based business recommendations;
* professional GitHub documentation.

## 6. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

* core KPIs can be reproduced consistently;
* data-quality issues and assumptions are documented;
* dashboard metrics are supported by validated calculations;
* churn-model performance is evaluated using appropriate classification metrics;
* customer segments are statistically defensible and commercially interpretable;
* recommendations are directly connected to analytical evidence;
* the repository can be understood and reproduced by another analyst.

## 7. Data Ethics and Limitations

The project uses synthetic portfolio data rather than confidential customer information.

Results should be presented as analytical findings from a business simulation and must not be represented as findings from a real company.

Predictive results should support business decision-making rather than be treated as guaranteed future outcomes.
